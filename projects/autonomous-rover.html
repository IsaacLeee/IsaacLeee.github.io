Project overview

Designed and fabricated an autonomous IR line-following rover as part of a six-person engineering team. I served as mechanical fabrication lead and helped with electronics.

Engineering objective

Build and integrate the mechanical and electronic hardware for an autonomous rover that follows an IR-detected line.

My role

Led mechanical fabrication and helped with electronics. Built polycarbonate and 3D-printed components and supported integration of motors, servos, sensors, a buck converter, and Arduino hardware.
