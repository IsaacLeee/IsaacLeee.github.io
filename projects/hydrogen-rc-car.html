Project overview

Developed a hydrogen-powered RC car for Hydrogen Grand Prix from September 2022 to June 2024. The work combined chassis design in Onshape, carbon fiber and aluminum fabrication, and electrical integration.

My contribution

  • Refined the chassis design in Onshape, reducing chassis mass by 14% from 1400 g to 1200 g and extending runtime per hydrogen canister by 10 minutes.
  • Fabricated carbon fiber and aluminum chassis using a laser cutter and CNC mill, producing durable, lightweight parts.
  • Implemented electrical systems through soldering and wiring, ensuring reliable operation across practice runs.
